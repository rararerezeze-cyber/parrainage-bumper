 Choose a referral from this page, copy the code or open the link, then follow the signup flow on Airbnb. Some programs apply the reward automatically from the link, while others ask you to enter the code during registration. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 Why did my Airbnb referral reward not appear? 
 

 
 
 
 Referral rewards can depend on your region, account status, campaign rules, and whether required first steps were completed. If a reward does not appear, check the terms inside Airbnb and contact their support with the referral details.
